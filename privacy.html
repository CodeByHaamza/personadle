We keep it honest.

PersonaDLE is a free fan-made guessing game. Here's everything you need to know about your data — no lawyer-speak.

🎮 100% Free 🇪🇺 GDPR 🚫 No Ads 📖 Open Source

📋 What we collect

  • 📧 Your email address — to create and secure your account
  • 👤 A username of your choice — displayed on your profile
  • 📊 Your game statistics — wins, streaks, attempts, time played
  • 🖼️ An avatar image — only if you choose to upload one
  • ⚙️ Your preferences — language, dark mode, theme, badges

🚫 What we never do

  • Sell your data — ever
  • Use it for advertising or profiling
  • Share it with third parties
  • Monetize anything — PersonaDLE is and will always be free

🔐 Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we literally cannot read them.
  • Sessions use a single secure, httpOnly cookie — no tracking.
  • The site runs on HTTPS in production.

⏳ Data retention

Your data is kept as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from your profile page — this triggers immediate anonymization followed by permanent deletion within 30 days.

⚖️ Your rights (GDPR)

  • Right of access — request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification — update your profile directly in the app
  • Right to deletion — use the "Delete my account" button in your profile
  • Right to portability — export your data with the "Export" button

🍪 Cookies

We use exactly one cookie: a technical session cookie (httpOnly, secure), strictly necessary for authentication. No advertising cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
