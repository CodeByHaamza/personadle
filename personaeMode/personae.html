👥 Personae Mode — How to Play

🎯 Goal:
A mysterious Persona appears on screen...
Your mission? Guess who it belongs to!
🧠 How it works:
  • 🖼️ A Persona is displayed — observe its design, motifs, and style.
  • 👤 Enter the name of the character you believe is the rightful user.
  • ✅ If correct, the result is revealed and counted as a win!
💡 Tips:
  • 🎨 Persona designs often reflect their user's personality, backstory, or arcana.
  • 🔄 Some Personae are shared by multiple characters — context is everything!
  • 📖 Use your lore knowledge from Persona 2, 3, 4, 5 and spin-offs.
❌ Give Up mechanic:
  • 🔁 After 3 failed attempts, the Give Up button becomes available.
  • 🆘 Clicking it reveals the correct character — but counts as a loss.
🎮 Games included:
Personae from P2 (IS & EP), P3, P4, P5, P5X and their respective spin-offs.
🔍 Trust your insight, rely on your memory, and prove your Persona mastery!
