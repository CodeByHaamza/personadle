1 PersonaDLE Version 1.1

Celebrate the Lunar New Year with exclusive content!

Welcome to Version 1.1 - featuring limited-time Chinese New Year event content, expanded Persona Q integration, new wallpapers across all games, enhanced Personae Mode with Picaro variants, and quality-of-life improvements. Ring in the Year of the Snake with style!

2 Chinese New Year Event

Limited-Time Event: January 25 - February 12, 2026

Celebrate the Lunar New Year with exclusive festive content! This special event brings traditional Chinese New Year aesthetics to PersonaDLE.

2.1 Event Content

2.1.1 All-Out Attack Mode - Festive Skins

Two exclusive Chinese New Year character skins available during the event:

Wonder (Chinese New Year)

Traditional Chinese New Year clothing with celebratory elements

Rin (Chinese New Year)

Festive outfit featuring traditional design elements

2.1.2 Event Rewards

Unlock exclusive rewards during the event period:

2.1.2.1 Chinese New Year Wallpaper

  • Type: Profile Wallpaper
  • Unlock: Automatically available during event period
  • Status: Permanent unlock

2.1.2.2 Chinese New Year Achievement Badge

  • Name: Chinese New Year Achievement
  • Unlock: Successfully identify both Wonder (CNY) and Rin (CNY) in All-Out Attack Mode
  • Status: Permanent unlock

2.1.2.3 CNY 2026 Attendee Badge

  • Code: CHINESNY2026
  • Unlock: Enter the event code in your profile
  • Status: Event-exclusive (January 25 - February 12, 2026)

Note: Event badges remain unlockable only during the event period. Achievement badge and wallpaper are permanent unlocks!

3 Special Event: P3R 2nd Anniversary

Celebrating 2 Years of Persona 3 Reload!

It has been two years since we returned to the Dark Hour. To celebrate this milestone, PersonaDLE is integrating the chaotic and stylish content from the ZUTOMAYO (Zutto Mayonaka de Iinoni) collaboration!

Three exclusive additions are now available to mark this anniversary:

3.0.1 1. New Special Track

Music Mode Expansion The airwaves have been hijacked! The exclusive mashup track “Mass Destruction - Reload (ZUTOMAYO Ver.)” has been added to the Music Mode playlist. * Featuring vocals by ACA-Ne and Lotus Juice.

3.0.2 2. New Achievement Badge

Hippocampus Reload * Condition: Successfully identify the ZUTOMAYO track in Music Mode. * Description: “Brainwaves synced! You found the chaotic mashup where growing pains meet mass destruction.”

3.0.3 3. Exclusive Profile Picture

Makoto Yuki (Zutomayo Ver.)

Wear the chaos! A unique avatar featuring the protagonist in the distinct ZUTOMAYO art style is now available. * Availability: Unlocked for everyone in Profile Settings. * Style: Based on the official collaboration MV art.

“Baby, baby, baby! The memory of this beat will never fade…”
