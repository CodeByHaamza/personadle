▼
  • 🎖️ NEW: Badge System! Unlock 19 unique achievements, including secret badges and event codes. Select up to 4 to showcase on your profile!
  • 🖼️ NEW: Profile Sharing! Share your stats with custom backgrounds or Persona-themed wallpapers. Click badge notifications to see details!
  • 🔊 NEW: Sound Effects! Added victory sound when winning and hover sound effects on all interactive buttons across all game modes.
  • 🌟 Added Fuuka Yamagishi to All-Out Attack mode.
  • ✨ Added Persona 5 Tactica characters (Erina, Toshiro).
  • 🎲 Improved randomization logic in All-Out Attack to reduce target repetition.

  • 🐞 Fixed Silhouette Mode image display issues.
  • 🐞 Fixed Classic Mode Error on Yukino Mazuki Data (the opus was P2EIS instead of P2IS).
  • ⚙️ Fixed Emoji Mode daily reset not triggering correctly.
  • 🎵 Fixed song title typos: "Your Are Stronger" → "You Are Stronger" and "The Days when my mother was there" → "When Mother Was There".
  • 🖼️ Added new profile pictures: Chidori, Tae, and Maruki.
  • ⏱️ Fixed time tracking calculation.
  • 🎨 Minor UI and dark mode adjustments for smoother experience.
