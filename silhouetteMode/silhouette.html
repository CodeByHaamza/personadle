🕶️ Silhouette Mode — How to Play

🎯 Goal:
A shadowy figure stands before you...
Can you identify the Persona character just from their black silhouette?
👤 How it works:
  • 🔍 The game starts with a heavily zoomed-in silhouette.
  • ❌ Every wrong guess zooms the image out, revealing more of the outline.
  • ✅ Find the right name, and the full portrait is revealed!
💡 Tips:
  • 🧍 Pay attention to the silhouette's pose, hairstyle, weapon, or accessories.
  • 👓 Some characters have distinct stances or iconic features — spot them!
  • 🎮 Use your knowledge from all Persona games and spin-offs.
📉 Give Up mechanic:
  • 🆘 The Give Up button unlocks after 5 failed attempts.
  • 🙈 You'll see the answer — but it counts as a loss!
🎮 Games included:
Characters from Persona 1, 2 (IS & EP), 3, 4, 5 and P5: The Phantom X including spin-offs.
🧠 Observe the shadows, trust your instincts, and become the ultimate silhouette detective!
⚡ Expert Mode:
The silhouette stays hidden. You only ever see it in flashes — and you decide when.
💡 Tips:
  • Press FLASH to show the silhouette for a fraction of a second.
  • You start with one flash, and every wrong guess earns you another one.
  • Each wrong guess also makes every following flash last longer.
  • No zoom here: the whole silhouette is shown — for as long as it lasts.
  • You get 5 tries before the Give Up button becomes available.
  • Expert rounds have their own daily answer, and do not count towards your streak.
